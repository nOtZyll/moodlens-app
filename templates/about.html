About MoodLens

MoodLens is an innovative Artificial Intelligence (AI)-based project designed to detect and recognize human facial emotions in real-time. The goal of this project is to help users better understand their own emotional spectrum and explore the psychological meaning behind each expression.

Behind the Technology

MoodLens first finds your face with the YuNet face detector. Two HSEmotion EfficientNet-B2 models, trained on the AffectNet dataset, then turn the face into a set of expression features. On top of them sits our own classifier, retrained on RAF-DB and AffectNet, which recognizes 7 core emotions: Happy, Sad, Angry, Fear, Disgust, Surprise, and Neutral. It reaches 85.8% accuracy on the RAF-DB test set, and all of it runs on this computer.
